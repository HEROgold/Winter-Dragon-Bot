Embeds next to Components V2

The cards you picked, each drawn twice: the embed version in the middle, and the same card built from Components V2 on the right. V2 support is tracked in #468.

In V2, a container is the box with the colored bar. A section is text with one thing on its right, a button or a thumbnail. Separators are the dividing lines. A V2 message can't hold an embed, and V2 has no side-by-side columns.

EmbedComponents V2

1. Sign-up, captains

In V2, each team is a section with its captain's avatar beside it, and the separators set the team list apart from the actions.

The buttons sit inside the container, so the card reads as one block.

There's no Participants button while everyone fits on the card. It only appears once the card stops listing everyone (past 40 components or 4000 characters), and the card then ends with "+6 more teams".

V2 is better here. The embed has no room for avatars per team.

3. Match card, drafting

V2 has no columns, so the teams stack. In exchange, "Manage match" moves up into the header section and becomes the first thing the organiser sees, and the heading can be larger than an embed title.

The draft turn the embed can't show gets its own line. Here, Snow Day's captain is the one holding the match up.

About even. The embed fits desktop better, V2 fits phones better, where inline fields stack anyway.

4. Live: the scoreboard

V2 can't put the scores side by side, so it stacks them like a broadcast score bar: one row per team, with the score in a large heading first.

During a live match, Manage match puts "Record game 3" at the top. It opens a short form asking for the winner and the map, and the bot ends the match once a team reaches the win count.

V2 is better here. Stacked rows read like a scoreboard on a phone as well.

5. Bracket

In V2 the image goes in a media gallery, which shows it at the container's full width. An embed image is drawn smaller.

Below the image, a button for each match being played opens that match's card for whoever clicks it, so nobody scrolls back through the channel to find it.

V2 is better here.

6. Vote

In V2, each answer is a section with a bar of its share of the votes and a Vote button beside it. The labels stay short however long the answer is, which matters because button labels stop at 80 characters.

The bar uses the step emoji. A closed vote drops the buttons and gives the winning answer the marker.

V2 is better here. It's the clearest of the vote designs.

7. Podium

The organiser sets the podium size per tournament: top 3, 5 or 10. This one is set to 5. The image always shows the three steps, and places 4 and up are listed below it.

Single elimination doesn't rank every team. Both losing semi-finalists finish "third", and all four losing quarter-finalists finish "fifth". This mock assumes a third-place match between the losing semi-finalists, so there's a real 3rd and 4th. Places from 5th down are always shared, so "top 5" here shows all four teams that finished fifth.

In V2, each listed team gets its captain's avatar.

Summary

V2 is better for 1, 2, 3b, 4, 5 and 6, about even for 3, and the same for 1b. Since a message is either V2 or an embed, I'd build every tournament card in V2 so they all look alike, once #468 is done.

The podium needs two new tournament settings: the podium size (3, 5 or 10), and whether a third-place match is played.
