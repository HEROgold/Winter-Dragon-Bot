PROTOTYPE: Clash cards

Three ways the Clash cog could post League of Legends Clash weekends. The bot checks Riot's schedule every Monday, makes a tournament per Clash day named like Worlds Clash Day 2, and posts in the channel set with /clash channel.

Every variant reuses /tournament in captains mode with teams of 5, and the lasting teams from /team. Riot plays the bracket in the client, so these tournaments never draw a bracket in Discord. Flip variants with the yellow bar or the arrow keys.

0. Setup, the same in every variant

/clash channel marks this channel. The reply shows what the weekly check found, so the admin sees at once that it works.

Mode picks Summoner's Rift, ARAM or both. Some servers only play one.

A. One post per day

Each Clash day is its own tournament and its own post, close to today's sign-up card. Captains enter a lasting team per day. The post changes as the day gets closer: sign-up, lock-in, results.

A1. Monday: the day's sign-up

The weekly check posts Day 1 and Day 2 as two messages. This is Day 2.

It's the existing captains sign-up card with a Clash header: the lock-in window as a Discord timestamp, so everyone sees their own time zone.

A team with 4 of 5 shows who's missing, because a Clash team can't lock in short.

Riot runs the bracket, so the bot can't know who won. Each captain clicks how far their team got, and the post becomes the day's results.

"Won" puts the marker on the team, like the podium card.

B. One board per Clash week

One message per Clash event, with a banner image. Each team is one row with a mark per day, so a team playing both days appears once. Behind it there are still two tournaments, one per day.

B1. The week's board

The banner is a Pillow image in a media gallery, like the bracket.

The day grid replaces two sign-up cards. "Enter my team" asks which team, then which days.

The board stays the one place to look all week, and the bot edits it as teams change.

C. Players first, teams drawn

For servers without fixed teams. Players say which days they can play. On Friday the organiser draws teams of 5 per day with /team's split, and the bot enters them as lasting teams so the captains can lock in.

C1. Monday: who can play?

Two toggle buttons per player, no captain needed. The counts say how many full teams each day can make, which is the reason to click.

A captain who already has a full team can still enter it, through the second row.

Open questions, for every variant

Riot's FAQ lists dates only. The lock-in times shown here are made up. The Riot API's Clash endpoint returns each day's registration and start time, and its name keys ("worlds", "day_2") give the names directly, but it needs a Riot API key.

Riot runs the bracket, so these tournaments skip /tournament start. They need a way to finish without a bracket, for example a new "external" flag on the tournament.
